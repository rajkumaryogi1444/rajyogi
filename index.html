About Shekhawat & Saini Hotel & Restaurant

Located on Nohar-Hisar Road, Gogamedi, Shekhawat & Saini Hotel & Restaurant offers clean & comfortable AC and Non-AC rooms with attached bathrooms for travelers and pilgrims. Our ground floor restaurant serves authentic Rajasthani & North Indian pure veg food. Managed by Sampat Singh Shekhawat & Krishan Kumar Saini. Perfect halt for families visiting Gogamedi Dham.
